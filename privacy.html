Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Who we are

Influmancer is a private social media management application operated by inQAsition ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the application processes when you use it or connect a social media account to it.

2. Data we process

  • User accounts: name, e-mail address, a salted password hash, timezone, and session data for invited users of the application.
  • Connected social media accounts: the platform's account identifier (e.g. TikTok open_id), username, display name, avatar URL, granted permission scopes, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the platform.
  • Content: media files, captions, hashtags and scheduling information that users create in the application, plus the identifiers of posts published through it.
  • Creator information: when publishing to TikTok, the application queries TikTok's creator_info endpoint to obtain the account's available privacy settings and limits, as TikTok requires.
  • Audit log: who performed which action in the application and when.

We do not collect data about followers, viewers or other third parties, and we do not read private messages.

3. Purpose

Data is processed solely to operate the Service: to authenticate users, to connect accounts the user owns or manages, to publish content that the user explicitly schedules, to check the status of those publications, and to keep a record of actions for security purposes.

4. Platform data and tokens

Access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and are used only to call the respective platform's official API on the user's behalf. We never ask for or store platform passwords. Tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected in the application; you can also revoke access at any time in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the social media platforms you connect, as required to publish your content, and to the infrastructure providers hosting the Service.

6. Retention

Data is kept for as long as the user account or connected social media account exists in the application, and deleted when it is removed. Audit log entries may be retained for up to 12 months.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting the operator (see below). Users can disconnect social media accounts themselves in the application.

8. Contact

Contact the operator through github.com/inQAsition.
